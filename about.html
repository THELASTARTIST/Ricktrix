Our Story

Imagine it’s 9 AM in Kolkata. You’re late for work, standing on a crowded street, trying to figure out which auto-rickshaw will take you to Salt Lake. You ask around, check your phone… but the routes are unclear, fares unpredictable, and travel time uncertain. Frustrating, right? This is exactly how AutoRoute was born. We realized millions of commuters face the same daily struggle. We thought: “Why should finding an auto route feel like a guessing game?” So, we built AutoRoute—a smart, community-driven platform that shows you: Which autos go where How much the ride costs Estimated travel time By combining technology with local knowledge, AutoRoute transforms the way people navigate the city—making auto travel predictable, affordable, and stress-free. Our mission is simple: empower every commuter with the confidence to travel smartly. And this is just the beginning—we plan to take AutoRoute beyond Kolkata, connecting cities across India.

We built AutoRoute to solve this problem. By combining technology with community knowledge, we've created a platform that makes auto travel predictable, affordable, and accessible for everyone.
